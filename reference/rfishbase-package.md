# The new R interface to Fishbase, v5.0

A programmatic interface to FishBase, re-written based on an
accompanying 'RESTful' API. Access tables describing over 30,000 species
of fish, their biology, ecology, morphology, and more. This package also
supports experimental access to SeaLifeBase data, which contains nearly
200,000 species records for all types of aquatic life not covered by
FishBase.'

## A message from the FishBase team

Thank you for using FishBase and SeaLifeBase through the rfishbase
package, representing more than 35 and 20 years of efforts,
respectively. With databases of such a large scope, both in terms of
species and life traits covered, one cannot expect that it is complete
and free of errors. We would be grateful if you could spare some of your
time, to directly report to FishBase and SeaLifeBase team:

- mistakes, typos, taxonomic issues, etc.;

- if you have completed the dataset on your own, by sending the pdfs or
  reference citations that would allow us to address some missing data;

- by sending articles that you have published using the data, pdfs or
  reference citations.

Please send your comments and corrections to <fishbase@q-quatics.org>.
You may send PDF files directly to the same address.
